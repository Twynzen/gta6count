# 🎮 Player Sound Effects

Place all player-related sound effects here in both MP3 and OGG formats.

## Required Files:

### Footsteps (4 variations)
- `sfx_footstep_01.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.15-0.25s)
- `sfx_footstep_02.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.15-0.25s)
- `sfx_footstep_03.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.15-0.25s)
- `sfx_footstep_04.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.15-0.25s)
- **Usage**: Played every 0.3-0.4s while moving
- **Volume**: -24dB to -20dB

### Shooting
- `sfx_shoot.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.1-0.2s)
- **Style**: Pistol/futuristic weapon
- **Volume**: -6dB to -3dB

### Multi-Shot (Upgraded)
- `sfx_multishot.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.15-0.25s)
- **Style**: Heavier/doubled shot sound
- **Volume**: -6dB to -3dB

### Player Hit (Damaged)
- `sfx_player_hit.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.3-0.5s)
- **Style**: Impact + pain indication
- **Volume**: -9dB to -6dB

### Player Death
- `sfx_player_death.mp3` / `.ogg` (1.0-1.5s)
- **Style**: Dramatic fall/impact
- **Volume**: -9dB to -6dB

## Specifications:
- **Format**: MP3 (128-192kbps) + OGG
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz
- **Channels**: Stereo

See `../../SOUND_ASSETS.md` for complete specifications.
