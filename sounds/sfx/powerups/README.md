# ✨ Power-Up Sound Effects

Place all power-up and ability-related sound effects here in both MP3 and OGG formats.

## Required Files:

### Power-Up Collection
- `sfx_powerup_appear.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.5-0.8s)
  - **Style**: Magical/celestial
  - **Volume**: -12dB to -9dB

- `sfx_powerup_collect.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.4-0.7s)
  - **Style**: Satisfying chime
  - **Volume**: -12dB to -9dB

### Ability Selection
- `sfx_roulette_tick.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.05-0.1s)
  - **Usage**: During roulette animation
  - **Volume**: -18dB to -15dB

- `sfx_ability_basic.mp3` / `.ogg` (1.0-1.5s)
  - **Style**: Cyan-colored sound (clear tones)
  - **Volume**: -6dB to -3dB

- `sfx_ability_epic.mp3` / `.ogg` (1.2-1.8s)
  - **Style**: Purple-colored sound (medium tones)
  - **Volume**: -6dB to -3dB

- `sfx_ability_legendary.mp3` / `.ogg` (1.5-2.5s)
  - **Style**: Golden-colored sound (epic!)
  - **Volume**: -6dB to -3dB

### Special Abilities
- `sfx_invisibility_on.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.5-0.8s)
  - **Style**: Magical/ghostly

- `sfx_invisibility_off.mp3` / `.ogg` (0.3-0.5s)
  - **Style**: Pop/fizzle

- `sfx_extra_life.mp3` / `.ogg` (1.0-1.5s)
  - **Style**: Positive fanfare
  - **Volume**: -9dB to -6dB

## Specifications:
- **Format**: MP3 (128-192kbps) + OGG
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz
- **Channels**: Stereo

See `../../SOUND_ASSETS.md` for complete specifications.
