# 🎵 Music Assets

Place all music files here in both MP3 and OGG formats.

## Required Files:

### 1. Main Menu Music
- `music_menu.mp3` (2:00-3:00 min loop)
- `music_menu.ogg`
- Style: Synthwave/Outrun/Miami Vice
- Loop: Perfect seamless loop
- Volume: -15dB to -12dB

### 2. Gameplay Music
- `music_game.mp3` (2:30-4:00 min loop)
- `music_game.ogg`
- Style: Synthwave/Action/Electronic
- BPM: 130-150
- Loop: Perfect seamless loop
- Volume: -18dB to -15dB

### 3. Game Over Music
- `music_gameover.mp3` (0:05-0:10 sec sting)
- `music_gameover.ogg`
- Style: Dramatic sting / Fail sound
- Volume: -12dB to -10dB

## Specifications:
- **Format**: MP3 (128-192kbps) + OGG (quality 5-7)
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz
- **Channels**: Stereo
- **Fade in/out**: 10-50ms in loops
